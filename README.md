In modern academic institutions, managing shared infrastructure—such as specialized research laboratories, high-performance computing equipment, and study spaces—presents significant administrative challenges. Traditional booking methods relying on manual logs, email chains, or physical sign-up sheets often lead to resource hoarding, double-booking conflicts, and poor visibility into facility utilization.

The Campus Resource & Lab Booking System is a full-stack web application designed to centralize and automate the reservation process for university equipment and spaces. By providing role-based access for students, faculty, and administrators, the system ensures equitable resource allocation, automates schedule conflict prevention, and delivers real-time analytics on laboratory usage.
